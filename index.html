01 / A little introduction

More than a
headline

I'm Paulina Torres Martínez — a Finance & Accounting graduate from Louisiana State University, now pursuing my M.S. in Business Analytics at Babson College, where I'm building on that foundation with data, strategy, and AI. I've always been fascinated by what drives performance, and how data can be used to improve it.

My academic and professional experience spans financial analysis, accounting, credit assessment, and market research — work that's sharpened my ability to read complex information, spot meaningful patterns, and translate data into insight that supports sound business decisions. I'm a CPA candidate, fluent in English, Spanish, and German, and an international student from Guatemala, which has shaped how I adapt, communicate, and think across cultures.

That same performance mindset carries into my life outside of work, from practicing karate to training for Ironman 70.3 triathlons. Ultimately, I'm drawn to the story behind the data — and to roles in finance, banking, investment, and consulting where I can turn that insight into meaningful action.
